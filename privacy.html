ChooseBrowser Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-08

ChooseBrowser is a macOS utility that lets you pick which installed browser opens a link. This policy describes what the app does with your data.

What the app collects

ChooseBrowser has no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no advertising, and it never sells or shares personal data.

The Mac App Store version collects and transmits nothing.

The direct-download version sends your licence key and the email address you used at checkout to Lemon Squeezy when you activate a licence, and re-checks that licence periodically. That is the only personal data it transmits, it goes nowhere else, and it happens only on the direct-download build.

What stays on your Mac

  • Links you open. When a link is routed through ChooseBrowser, it is handed directly to the browser you choose. The link is not logged to disk and is never sent anywhere else.
  • Routing rules and preferences. Rules like "always open this site in Firefox", your browser order, hidden browsers, and the fallback browser are stored locally in the app's own sandbox container on your Mac.
  • Installed browsers. The app asks macOS which installed applications can open web links so it can list them. This list is never uploaded.

Network access

The Mac App Store version of ChooseBrowser makes no network connections of its own. It has no network entitlement. Updates are delivered through the Mac App Store.

The direct-download version connects to api.lemonsqueezy.com to activate and periodically verify your licence, and may check api.github.com for a newer release. Neither request carries anything beyond your licence key, your purchase email and what any HTTPS request carries.

Third parties

ChooseBrowser does not use third-party SDKs or services.

Children

ChooseBrowser is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at the same URL and the date above will be revised.
