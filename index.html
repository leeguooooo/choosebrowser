ChooseBrowser

Pick a browser for every link.

ChooseBrowser is a small macOS utility. Set it as your default browser once, and every http/https link you click shows a chooser listing the browsers installed on your Mac. Pick one with a click or ⌘1–⌘9, and the link opens there.

Features

  • Lists every installed browser automatically
  • Per-site rules, ordered failover, or open in several browsers at once
  • Reorder and hide browsers; set a fallback
  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics

Get it

Download ChooseBrowser — a notarized .dmg, free to try for 7 days. After that, a licence is US$4.99, a one-time purchase that activates up to 3 Macs.

Or get it on the Mac App Store.

Requires macOS 26 or newer.
